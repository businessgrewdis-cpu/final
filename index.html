// WHO WE ARE

We Are Grewdis

Grewdis is a next-generation Influencer Marketing & Digital Branding Agency based in Patna, Bihar — but our reach is pan-India and growing global. We believe every brand has a story worth telling, and we find the perfect creator voices to tell it.

From micro-influencers with hyper-engaged communities to macro creators with massive reach, we curate data-driven campaigns that generate authentic connections between brands and audiences. Our team lives at the intersection of creativity, technology, and strategy.

Whether you're a startup looking to make noise or an established brand ready to dominate — Grewdis turns attention into action.
